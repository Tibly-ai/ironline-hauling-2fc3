FAQ

Straight answers before the load moves.

Shippers ask us the same handful of questions before they book a haul with Ironline Trailer Hauling. Here they are, answered plainly, no fine print buried in a quote email.

Ask Us Directly See Our Services

Service Area & Equipment

Where we run, and what we run it on.

What areas does Ironline cover? +

We're based out of Amarillo, Texas and run a regular radius covering the Texas Panhandle, eastern New Mexico, western Oklahoma, and southern Colorado. We also run scheduled lanes into Dallas-Fort Worth and Denver most weeks. Outside that footprint, call us. If it lines up with a backhaul, we'll quote it.

What trailer types do you run? +

Flatbed, dry van, refrigerated (reefer), and step-deck. Flatbed and step-deck cover steel, machinery, and lumber. Dry van handles palletized freight that needs to stay dry. Reefer keeps produce and cold chain shipments at temperature door to door.

Can you handle oversized or overweight loads? +

Yes. Our step-deck rigs pull oversized freight up to 12 feet wide and 48,000 lbs. We handle the state permits and line up a pilot car when the load requires one, so you're not chasing paperwork on your end.

Booking & Scheduling

How a load goes from a phone call to a truck at your dock.

How do I book a haul with Ironline? +

Call dispatch or send the details through our contact form: pickup and drop-off addresses, weight, dimensions, and the date you need it moved. We'll quote a rate within a couple hours and confirm a pickup window once you sign off.

How far in advance should I schedule a pickup? +

Same-day and next-day pickups are routine on our regular lanes if a truck's already headed that direction. For oversized freight or a multi-stop run, give us 48 to 72 hours so we can clear permits and line up a pilot car if needed.

Do you guarantee on-time delivery? +

We quote a firm pickup and delivery window at booking and we run our own trucks with our own drivers, no subcontracted capacity to lose track of. If weather or a mechanical issue is going to push the window, dispatch calls you before you have to call us.

Insurance & Dispatch

What's covered, and when someone's actually on the phone.

What insurance coverage do you carry? +

Every load runs with $1,000,000 in auto liability and $100,000 in standard cargo coverage. Higher cargo limits are available for high-value freight, just ask when you get your quote. A certificate of insurance goes out with every booking at no extra charge.

What are your dispatch hours? +

Dispatch is staffed Monday through Saturday, 5:00 AM to 7:00 PM. Loads that break down outside those hours or need an emergency haul get answered by our on-call line, 24 hours a day.

What happens if there's a delay or breakdown? +

You get a call from dispatch as soon as we know, with an updated ETA and options if you need to make other arrangements. We also keep working relationships with two other regional carriers so a truck can be swapped in on short notice when a load absolutely has to move.

Didn't see your question?

Send us the details of your load and we'll get you a straight answer and a real quote, not a form letter. Or call dispatch directly during business hours.
